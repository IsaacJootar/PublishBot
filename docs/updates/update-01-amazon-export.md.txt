## Change 1 — Export (replace plain text output)

The digital products export must produce THREE files not plain text:

**File 1: premium.pdf** (for Gumroad / Selar / Payhip)
- Cover page: product title, author name, tagline, brand colours
- Auto-generated table of contents
- Each section as heading, items formatted cleanly underneath
- Page numbers in footer
- Convert via existing ExportService.php using Pandoc

**File 2: kdp-version.docx** (for Amazon KDP upload)
- Times New Roman 12pt body text
- 1 inch margins all sides
- Chapter-based structure
- No custom headers or footers
- Ready to upload directly to kdp.amazon.com

**File 3: master.docx** (user keeps for editing)
- Same as premium version but editable DOCX

Save all three to:
storage/app/users/{user_id}/digital-products/{product_id}/

Show three download buttons on Stage 5:

## Change 2 — Platform upload instructions (Stage 5)

Amazon KDP is a PRIMARY platform. Add full KDP upload steps:
1. Go to kdp.amazon.com and sign in
2. Click Add new title → Kindle ebook OR Paperback
3. Fill title and description from the listing above
4. Upload kdp-version.docx as the manuscript
5. Upload a cover image (Canva or Midjourney)
6. Set Kindle price $9.99 · Paperback price $19.99
7. Click Publish — live within 24-72 hours
8. Set up Payoneer at payoneer.com to receive royalties

Show platform pricing clearly on Stage 5:
Gumroad / Selar: $37–$147 · Amazon Kindle: $9.99 · Paperback: $19.99

All upload instructions use orange "Your action needed" badge.
